Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. How we use cookies and local storage technology.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you browse websites. They help remember your preferences, enhance site navigation, and enable key interactive features.

2. How We Use Cookies & Local Storage

Discover Hidden Morocco uses cookies and browser local storage for the following essential purposes:

  • Theme Preferences: Saving your chosen Light Mode or Dark Mode theme settings across pages.
  • Language Selection: Remembering your selected language preference (EN, FR, ES).
  • Wishlist & Favorites: Storing saved destinations in your personal wishlist so you can revisit them anytime.
  • Analytics & Performance: Understanding visitor interactions to continually optimize page loading times and spatial map features.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates trusted third-party resources such as Leaflet maps and FontAwesome icons. These providers may set technical cookies necessary for rendering spatial maps or font icons.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or disable cookies directly through your web browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to save wishlist items or preserve theme preferences.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy, please reach out via email to contact@discoverhiddenmorocco.com.
